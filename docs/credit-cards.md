# Credit cards

A credit card is an account of type **Credit card**. Create one on the **Accounts** page, or turn an existing account into one.

## In the account form

Choose the type **Credit card** and fill in the card terms:

- **Credit limit**: above zero, in the currency of the account.
- **Statement day**: the day of the month the statement closes (1 to 31).
- **Grace period**: days after the statement to pay it (0 to 60).

To turn a bank, cash or crypto account into a card, open it, switch the type to **Credit card** and save. Its name, currency and balance stay as they are. Only the statement-day cycle exists: a statement always closes on a fixed day.

## In the account list

A card row looks like a debt or a budget: what is owed, "of" the limit, and a thin bar under it that fills with the share of the limit used. The bar is green while all is well, yellow when a payment is due and red when the card is overdue or over its limit.

A yellow or red triangle next to the name says why. Click or tap it to read the reason, for example "Pay 400 USD by Nov 18".
